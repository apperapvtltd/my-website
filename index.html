AppEra is a Hyderabad-based training and career support company. We teach students, job seekers and company teams how to use real business software like CRM, accounting, HRMS and ATS, help candidates get placed through the #callHR program, and guide startup founders on funding readiness.

No. The training is beginner friendly and needs no coding. We start with what each application does and why companies use it, then move to hands-on practice using real business examples.

You learn the software used across departments: CRM for sales and customers, accounting and books, HRMS and payroll, ATS for recruitment, marketing tools, project management, operations and LMS. You also learn how these tools connect inside a real company workflow.

#callHR teaches you to build a strong resume and portfolio, find the right HR contacts, send outreach that gets replies, follow up properly and prepare for interviews. It replaces random applying with a repeatable process that creates more interview calls.

Anyone can join: students, freshers, job seekers, working professionals who want to upskill, freelancers, and company teams that need training on the tools they already use.
